# Social brand icons

Instagram, Telegram, X, YouTube and Aparat SVGs come from Simple Icons:
https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/develop/icons

Simple Icons distributes icons under CC0. Brand trademarks remain their owners' property.

Bale is sourced from https://bale.ai/logo/bale_logo.svg; its original symbol path is retained and the viewBox isolates the symbol from the wordmark.

The footer places these local vector assets below the press permit and uses CSS masks to render them in white. All marks reveal brand colors and lift slightly on hover, respecting reduced-motion preferences. X uses a white circular backdrop for contrast. Only Instagram has a configured destination; other marks do not navigate until their URLs are supplied.
